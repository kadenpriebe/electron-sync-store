START HERE

Two windows, one shared piece of state. The box on top owns that state and is the only thing allowed to change it. Each window keeps its own copy.

A window never edits its copy. It asks the owner, the owner decides, and the owner sends the result down to every window.

Try it: click + in either window and watch the other one.

Now set message speed to very slow and click again. The window you clicked changes instantly, long before the message has reached the owner. It is guessing, and it is nearly always right. Press make the owner say no and click: this guess is wrong, and the number snaps back the moment the answer arrives.

Then press 50 clicks at once. The owner answers one ask at a time, so its answers fall behind — and the numbers on the pages never slow down at all. That is what the guessing is for.
