This is a live look inside a small library that keeps one piece of state identical across the separate processes of an Electron app.

The box on top is the process that owns the state. The two boxes below are two real windows; each holds a copy. Windows never change their copy directly. They send a message up, the owner decides, and the owner sends the result back down to everyone.

Try it: click + in either window and watch the other one.
